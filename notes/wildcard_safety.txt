What * meant in my pattern:
The * wildcard matched any characters after "data" and before ".txt". Therefore, data*.txt matched data1.txt, data2.txt, and data3.txt.

Why I listed matches before copying:
I listed the matching files first to confirm exactly which files the wildcard selected. This helped me verify that only the intended data files would be copied.

Why wildcards are dangerous with delete commands:
Wildcards can match more files than expected if the pattern is too broad. Using a wildcard with a delete command could remove multiple important files at once. I should always check the matches carefully before using wildcards with destructive commands.
